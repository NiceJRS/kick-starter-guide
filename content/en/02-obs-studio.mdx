<TipBox>
On Windows, always right-click the OBS Studio icon and select <strong>Run as administrator</strong> before streaming. This gives OBS GPU priority over background tasks, preventing game frame drops.
</TipBox>

<SectionHeading id="s2-2">2.2 Output Settings (Encoder & Bitrate)</SectionHeading>

<StepCard step={2} title="Unlock Advanced Settings & Select Your Encoder">
  <SubSteps items={[
    "In OBS Studio, navigate to <strong>Settings → Output</strong>.",
    "Change the <strong>Output Mode</strong> dropdown from 'Simple' to <strong>Advanced</strong> to unlock precise controls.",
    "Under the Streaming tab, select your GPU <strong>Encoder</strong>: <strong>NVIDIA NVENC H.264</strong> (NVIDIA GeForce RTX/GTX), <strong>AMD HW H.264</strong> (AMD GPUs), <strong>Apple VT H.264</strong> (Apple Silicon Macs), or <strong>x264</strong> (software CPU encoding — no dedicated GPU)."
  ]} />
</StepCard>

<StepCard step={3} title="Configure Bitrate & Resolution Parameters">
  <SubSteps items={[
    "<strong>Rate Control:</strong> Set to <strong>CBR (Constant Bitrate)</strong> to maintain a stable stream signal.",
    "<strong>Keyframe Interval:</strong> Set to <strong>2s</strong> (standard requirement for streaming platforms).",
    "<strong>Preset:</strong> Select <strong>P5: Slow (Good Quality)</strong> or <strong>Quality</strong>.",
    "Navigate to <strong>Settings → Video</strong>. Set <strong>Base (Canvas) Resolution</strong> to match your monitor (e.g., 1920x1080) and set <strong>Output (Scaled) Resolution</strong> according to your target stream quality."
  ]} />
  <ImageCaption src="/images/ch2/s2_2_step2_output_settings.jpg" alt="OBS Advanced Output Settings" caption="Advanced Output Settings and Bitrate configuration in OBS Studio" />
</StepCard>

<BitrateTable locale="en" />

<WarningBox>
Bitrate acts like pipe bandwidth — higher bitrate means clearer video. Your home internet Upload speed should always be at least <strong>1.5× your configured bitrate</strong> to avoid dropped frames.
</WarningBox>

<SectionHeading id="s2-faq">FAQ</SectionHeading>

<FaqItem q="Why is my stream lagging despite having fast upload speed?">
GPU/CPU overload is the most common cause. Lower Output Resolution to 720p60, switch Encoder Preset from P5 to P4/P3, and launch OBS Studio as Administrator.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem q="What is the maximum recommended Bitrate for KICK?">
KICK recommends a maximum of 8,000 Kbps for 1080p 60fps. Official guidelines are available at [KICK Help Center](https://help.kick.com).
</FaqItem>
