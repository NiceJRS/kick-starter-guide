**Q: Why can't mobile app viewers find the Subscribe button on KICK?**  
A: To avoid Apple and Google's 15%–30% In-App Purchase fees and preserve the 95/5 revenue split, KICK restricts subscriptions to web browsers. Set up a `!sub` chatbot command with a Direct Subscription URL (`https://kick.com/[username]/subscribe`) and share it in chat and Discord.

**Q: Can viewers without a credit card subscribe on KICK?**  
A: Yes. Thai viewers can open the TrueMoney Wallet app, generate a virtual **TrueMoney Mastercard** (16-digit card number + CVV), and use those card details to subscribe via web browser.
