**Q: Why didn't a viewer receive the @Subscriber Discord role automatically after subscribing on KICK?**  
A: This usually happens because the viewer hasn't linked their KICK and Discord accounts via BotRix yet. Instruct viewers to pair accounts on botrix.live or type `!sync` in chat, or manually assign the `@Subscriber` role in Discord as a fallback.

**Q: Why shouldn't I use the @everyone tag in Go-Live notification messages?**  
A: Tagging `@everyone` every time you go live causes notification fatigue, leading viewers to mute your server. Create an opt-in `@Live Alert` role in a `#get-roles` channel and tag only that role instead.
