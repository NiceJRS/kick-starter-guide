<SectionHeading id="s4-2">4.2 Banned Words & Auto-Moderation</SectionHeading>

<StepCard step={4} title="Add Banned Words">
  <SubSteps items={[
    "Go to <a href='https://dashboard.kick.com/moderation/banned-words' target='_blank'>dashboard.kick.com/moderation/banned-words</a>.",
    "Type a prohibited term in the <strong>Add banned word</strong> field and press Enter.",
    "<strong>Recommended baseline terms:</strong> profanity, hate speech, and gambling/scam spam.",
    "Example scam keywords: <code>casino</code>, <code>slot</code>, <code>pgslot</code>, <code>directweb</code>, <code>deposit-withdraw</code>, <code>line@</code>.",
    "Messages containing these words are <strong>silently blocked before they appear in chat</strong> — viewers never see them."
  ]} />
  <ImageCaption src="/images/ch4/s4_2_step4_banned_words_list.jpg" alt="Banned Words input field" caption="Moderation → Banned Words — adding prohibited terms to the filter list" />
</StepCard>

<StepCard step={5} title="Wildcard Asterisk (*) Technique">
  <SubSteps items={[
    "Wrap a keyword with <code>*</code> to catch all variations containing that root word.",
    "Example: <code>*slot*</code> catches <code>pgslot</code>, <code>slotgame</code>, <code>freeslots</code>, etc.",
    "Example: <code>*casino*</code> catches <code>onlinecasino</code>, <code>casinobonus</code>, etc.",
    "Without wildcards, viewers can easily bypass filters by adding prefixes or suffixes."
  ]} />
  <ImageCaption src="/images/ch4/s4_2_step5_wildcard_technique.jpg" alt="Wildcard asterisk technique" caption="Wildcard technique — *slot* catches all variations like pgslot, slotgame, freeslots" />
</StepCard>

<StepCard step={6} title="Block Disruptive Emotes">
  <SubSteps items={[
    "KICK emotes are encoded as <code>[emote:ID:EmoteName]</code> behind the scenes — e.g., <code>[emote:39251:beeBobble]</code>.",
    "To ban a disruptive emote: type the emote name <code>beeBobble</code> into the Banned Words field.",
    "Or use wildcard: <code>*beeBobble*</code> to catch all variations.",
    "Click <strong>Add</strong> — any message containing that emote will be blocked before it reaches chat."
  ]} />
  <ImageCaption src="/images/ch4/s4_2_step6_block_emote.jpg" alt="Blocking emote via Banned Words" caption="Blocking disruptive emotes — add emote name to Banned Words to silently filter them" />
</StepCard>

<WarningBox>
<strong>Banned Words vs "Deleted by Moderator":</strong> Banned Words block messages before they ever appear in chat. If you see "deleted by moderator," that message was removed after being posted — either manually by a mod or retroactively by a chatbot like BotRix.
</WarningBox>

---

<FaqItem q="How can I block scam links while still allowing Subscribers to share links?">
KICK's native Block Links toggle applies globally to all non-mod viewers. For Subscriber-only link permissions, configure URL Protection via BotRix or Nightbot (Chapter 5).
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem q="Why are viewers still bypassing banned words with prefixes/suffixes?">
Use wildcard asterisks `*` around the keyword — e.g., `*casino*` instead of `casino`. This catches any message containing the root word.
</FaqItem>
