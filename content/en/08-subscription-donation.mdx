<FaqItem q="Why can't mobile app viewers find the Subscribe button on KICK?">
To avoid Apple and Google's 15%–30% In-App Purchase fees and preserve the 95/5 revenue split, KICK restricts subscriptions to web browsers. Set up a `!sub` chatbot command with a Direct Subscription URL (`https://kick.com/[username]/subscribe`) and share it in chat and Discord.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem q="Can viewers without a credit card subscribe on KICK?">
Yes. Thai viewers can open the TrueMoney Wallet app, generate a virtual **TrueMoney Mastercard** (16-digit card number + CVV), and use those card details to subscribe via web browser.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem q="What do I do if Stripe verification fails?">
Most failures are caused by an English name mismatch or incomplete W-8 form. Go to dashboard.stripe.com/login with the same email → Settings → fix Display Name to English matching your ID → fix W-8 (tick No for U.S. person) → add English signature → resubmit. You will receive a "Your identity has been verified!" email when approved.
</FaqItem>
