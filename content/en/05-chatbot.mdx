<FaqItem q="Why is my chatbot not responding or deleting spam in live chat?">
Ensure you have granted Moderator status to `KickBot` or `BotRix` at [dashboard.kick.com/community/moderators](https://dashboard.kick.com/community/moderators). Without moderator privileges, the bot cannot send messages or purge chat spam.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem q="Can I run both KickBot and BotRix at the same time?">
Yes. A recommended setup: use KickBot for AI TTS voice reading + !clip tools, and BotRix for OBS Alerts, subscriber-exempt link protection, and the Now Playing music overlay.
</FaqItem>
