<SectionHeading id="s1-1">1. Registration & Security</SectionHeading>

<StepCard step={1} title="Sign Up">
  Visit [KICK.com](https://kick.com) and click **Sign Up**.
  <SubSteps items={[
    "Go to <strong>kick.com</strong> in your browser",
    "Click the <strong>Sign Up</strong> button (top-right)",
    "Choose <strong>Google OAuth</strong> (faster) or <strong>Email / Password</strong>"
  ]} />
</StepCard>

<ImageCaption
  src="/images/ch1/1_1_0_signup.jpg"
  alt="KICK.com sign up page"
  caption="KICK Sign Up page — choose Google OAuth or email/password"
/>

<StepCard step={2} title="Email Verification">
  Confirm your email before continuing.
  <SubSteps items={[
    "Check your <strong>Inbox</strong> for an email from KICK",
    "If not found, check <strong>Spam / Junk</strong>",
    "Click the verification link or enter the <strong>OTP code</strong>"
  ]} />
</StepCard>

<ImageCaption
  src="/images/ch1/1_1_1_email_verify_otp.jpg"
  alt="Email verification OTP"
  caption="Enter the OTP code sent to your email"
/>

<StepCard step={3} title="Enable 2FA (Two-Factor Authentication)">
  Secure your account — required to unlock monetization.
  <SubSteps items={[
    "Go to <strong>Creator Dashboard → Settings → Security</strong>",
    "Find the <strong>Two-Factor Authentication</strong> section",
    "Click <strong>Enable</strong> to start the setup"
  ]} />
</StepCard>

<ImageCaption
  src="/images/ch1/1_1_2_profile_setting_activate_2fa_page.jpg"
  alt="Security settings page to activate 2FA"
  caption="Navigate to Settings → Security to find the 2FA toggle"
/>

<StepCard step={4} title="Set Up Authenticator App">
  Install an authenticator app on your phone to generate codes.
  <SubSteps items={[
    "Download <strong>Google Authenticator</strong> or <strong>Authy</strong> from the App Store / Play Store",
    "Open the app — you will use it to scan a QR Code in the next step"
  ]} />
</StepCard>

<ImageCaption
  src="/images/ch1/1_1_3_application_google_authenticator.jpg"
  alt="Google Authenticator app"
  caption="Use Google Authenticator or Authy to generate TOTP codes"
/>

<StepCard step={5} title="Scan QR Code & Verify">
  Link KICK to your authenticator app and confirm it works.
  <SubSteps items={[
    "Open your authenticator app and tap <strong>Add account / Scan QR</strong>",
    "Scan the QR Code shown on KICK's Security page",
    "Enter the <strong>6-digit code</strong> from the app to confirm"
  ]} />
</StepCard>

<ImageCaption
  src="/images/ch1/1_1_4_verify_2fa_scan_qr_code.jpg"
  alt="Scan QR code to verify 2FA"
  caption="Scan the QR Code with your authenticator app"
/>

<ImageCaption
  src="/images/ch1/1_1_5_verify_2fa_success.jpg"
  alt="2FA setup success"
  caption="2FA enabled successfully — your account is now secured"
/>

<TipBox>
  2FA is **mandatory** on KICK to unlock Monetization tools and receive payouts. Enable it before your first stream.
</TipBox>

<SectionHeading id="s1-3">3. Stream Key & Server URL</SectionHeading>

<StepCard step={1} title="Open Creator Dashboard">
  Access the streaming settings area.
  <SubSteps items={[
    "Click your profile icon (top-right) and select <strong>Creator Dashboard</strong>",
    "Or go to <strong>kick.com/dashboard</strong> directly"
  ]} />
</StepCard>

<ImageCaption
  src="/images/ch1/1_3_0_creator_dashboard.jpg"
  alt="Creator Dashboard"
  caption="Creator Dashboard — your streaming control center"
/>

<StepCard step={2} title="Find Your Stream Key">
  Copy both the Server URL and Stream Key — you need both for OBS.
  <SubSteps items={[
    "Navigate to <strong>Settings → Stream Key</strong> (or <strong>Channel → Stream URL &amp; Key</strong>)",
    "Copy <strong>Stream URL (RTMP):</strong> <code>rtmps://fa723fc1b171.global-contribute.live-video.net:443/app</code>",
    "Click <strong>Show</strong> next to Stream Key, then copy it (requires 2FA)"
  ]} />
</StepCard>

<ImageCaption
  src="/images/ch1/1_3_1_stream_key_page.jpg"
  alt="Stream key settings page"
  caption="Your Stream URL and Stream Key — copy these into OBS"
/>

<ImageCaption
  src="/images/ch1/1_3_2_click_show_stream_url_need_f2a.jpg"
  alt="Show stream URL requires 2FA"
  caption="Revealing the Stream Key requires 2FA to be enabled"
/>

<WarningBox>
  **Never show your Stream Key on screen during a stream.** If it gets leaked, click **Reset Key** immediately to generate a new one.
</WarningBox>

<StepCard step={3} title="Add to OBS Studio">
  Paste both values into OBS to connect your stream to KICK.
  <SubSteps items={[
    "Open OBS Studio → <strong>Settings → Stream</strong>",
    "Set <strong>Service</strong> to <strong>Custom</strong>",
    "Paste the <strong>Stream URL</strong> into the Server field",
    "Paste your <strong>Stream Key</strong> into the Stream Key field",
    "Click <strong>Apply → OK</strong> to save"
  ]} />
</StepCard>

<ImageCaption
  src="/images/ch1/1_3_3_obs_studio_add_stream_key.jpg"
  alt="OBS Studio stream key configuration"
  caption="Paste your KICK Stream URL and Key into OBS Settings → Stream"
/>

<SectionHeading id="s1-4">4. Monetization & KICK Affiliate</SectionHeading>

<AffiliateRequirements locale="en" />

<StepCard step={1} title="Claim Affiliate">
  Officially activate your Affiliate status on KICK.
  <SubSteps items={[
    "Go to <strong>Creator Dashboard → Achievements</strong>",
    "Find the Affiliate badge and click <strong>Claim</strong>"
  ]} />
</StepCard>

<StepCard step={2} title="Set Up Payout">
  Access the Stripe payout configuration.
  <SubSteps items={[
    "Navigate to <strong>Creator Dashboard → Monetization / Payout</strong>",
    "Click <strong>Set Up Payout</strong> to start the Stripe Connect flow"
  ]} />
</StepCard>

<StepCard step={3} title="KYC Verification">
  Verify your identity so Stripe can process payments.
  <SubSteps items={[
    "Prepare your <strong>Thai ID card</strong> or <strong>Passport</strong>",
    "Upload front and back photos as prompted by Stripe",
    "Wait for verification — usually completed within 1–2 business days"
  ]} />
</StepCard>

<StepCard step={4} title="Link Thai Bank Account">
  Connect your bank to receive THB payouts directly.
  <SubSteps items={[
    "Enter your Thai bank account number and bank name",
    "Confirm account details — payouts arrive in <strong>THB</strong>",
    "Payout cycle is approximately <strong>7 business days</strong> after each payment period"
  ]} />
</StepCard>

<SectionHeading id="s1-faq">FAQ</SectionHeading>

<FaqItem q="What documents are needed for Thai streamer payouts?">
A Thai Government ID or Passport for Stripe KYC, plus a Thai bank account to receive THB payouts.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem q="Can I stream without 2FA?">
Yes — but 2FA is required to access Monetization settings and receive any income from subs or donations.
</FaqItem>
