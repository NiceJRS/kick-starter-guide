<FaqItem q="Can Moderators initiate Predictions or edit stream details?">
Yes. Mods can run `/prediction`, start `/poll`, execute `/raid`, and update stream title/category via the Creator Dashboard.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem q="How do I find out which Moderator banned or timed out a viewer?">
Check the Mod Actions Log — open `https://dashboard.kick.com/popout/[username]/mod-actions` (replace `[username]` with your channel name). Every action is logged with the mod's name and timestamp.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem q="How do I unban someone who was previously banned?">
Type `/unban [username]` in live chat, or go to Creator Dashboard → Moderation → Banned users and remove them from the list.
</FaqItem>
