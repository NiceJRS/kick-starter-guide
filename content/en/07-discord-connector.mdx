<FaqItem q="Why didn't a viewer receive the @Subscriber Discord role automatically after subscribing on KICK?">
This usually happens because the viewer hasn't signed in through the RoleLogic verification page to link their KICK and Discord accounts yet. Ask them to sign in again, or manually assign the `@Subscriber` role in Discord as a fallback.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem q="Why shouldn't I use the @everyone tag in Go-Live notification messages?">
Tagging `@everyone` every time you go live causes notification fatigue, leading viewers to mute your server. Create an opt-in `@Live Alert` role in a `#get-roles` channel and tag only that role instead.
</FaqItem>
