<WarningBox>
<strong>Banned Words vs "Deleted by Moderator":</strong> Banned Words block messages before they ever appear in chat. If you see "deleted by moderator," that message was removed after being posted — either manually by a mod or retroactively by a chatbot like BotRix.
</WarningBox>

<FaqItem q="How can I block scam links while still allowing Subscribers to share links?">
KICK's native Block Links toggle applies globally to all non-mod viewers. For Subscriber-only link permissions, configure URL Protection via BotRix (BotRix Functions).
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem q="Why doesn't Banned Words catch every variation of a spam term?">
KICK's Banned Words filter only matches exact text and doesn't support wildcards. For more flexible filtering, use BotRix or KickBot instead.
</FaqItem>
