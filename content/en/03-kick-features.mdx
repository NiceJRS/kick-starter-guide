**Q: How long are VODs kept?**  
A: 60 days automatically. No setup needed — manage them at Studio → VODs.

**Q: Where are Channel Points and Emotes settings?**  
A: Emotes → `Community → Chat → Emotes` | Channel Points → `Community → Chat → Channel Points`.
