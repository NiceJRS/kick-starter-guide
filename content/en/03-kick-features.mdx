**Q: How long are stream VODs stored on KICK?**  
A: Stream VODs are automatically archived and stored for 60 days. You can manage and download them at Creator Dashboard → Studio → Videos.

**Q: Where can I find the settings for Channel Points and Emotes?**  
A: Emote settings are located at `Community → Emotes`, while Channel Points configuration is under `Community → Chat → Channel points`.
