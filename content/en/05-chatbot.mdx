**Q: Why is my chatbot not responding or deleting spam in live chat?**  
A: Ensure you have granted Moderator status to `KickBot` or `BotRix` at [dashboard.kick.com/community/moderators](https://dashboard.kick.com/community/moderators). Without moderator privileges, the bot cannot send messages or purge chat spam.

**Q: Can I run both KickBot and BotRix at the same time?**  
A: Yes. A recommended setup: use KickBot for AI TTS voice reading + !clip tools, and BotRix for OBS Alerts, subscriber-exempt link protection, and the Now Playing music overlay.
